Channel Uploader

A private, single-user tool for publishing videos to one YouTube channel.

What it does

Channel Uploader is a personal desktop script that uploads video files the owner already created to the owner's own YouTube channel, with a title, description and optional scheduled publish time. It is not distributed, not offered as a service, and has no users other than the channel owner.

How it uses Google data

  • Signs in with Google OAuth to identify the channel owner.
  • Uploads video files chosen by the owner to that same channel.
  • Never reads, modifies or deletes anything the owner did not explicitly submit.

Privacy

Credentials stay on the owner's machine. No user data is collected, shared, sold or transmitted to any third party. See the privacy policy for details.
