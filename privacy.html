Privacy Policy

Channel Uploader · Last updated: 27 October 2026

Channel Uploader is a personal, single-user desktop tool used by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. This policy explains what it accesses and how that information is handled.

Information accessed

  • Google account identity — used only to confirm which channel is being posted to.
  • YouTube upload permission (youtube.upload) — used only to upload videos the owner supplies.
  • Read-only channel metadata (youtube.readonly) — channel name and basic stats, shown after sign-in for confirmation.

How information is stored

OAuth credentials are stored locally on the owner's computer in a token file. They are never uploaded to a server, and no analytics, tracking or telemetry is collected.

Sharing

No data is sold, rented, shared or transferred to third parties. The tool communicates only with Google's YouTube Data API, as required to perform uploads.

Retention and deletion

Stored credentials remain on the owner's machine until deleted. Removing the local token file deletes all stored authorizations; access can also be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page (myaccount.google.com/permissions).

Limited use

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the site owner via the contact address on the home page.
